Title: one plain question about something familiar, under 60 characters, casual Saudi, no clickbait lies, no emoji needed.
Line 1: the surprising fact or the question itself, spoken in under 4 seconds, full tashkeel, avoid words with ق when a natural alternative exists.
Give 5 title options and 3 first-line options ranked best first, with one short reason each. Keep the facts exactly as given. Never edit files.
